# Roadmap

Central runs on the August ERP base, copied in once. The previous system (in this
repository's history before that commit) had features the base does not; they come onto
the base in the order below, one sub-project at a time. Each sub-project gets a spec in
`docs/superpowers/specs/` and an implementation plan before any code, and lands in the
client layer (`app/Client`, `config/client.php`) except where CLAUDE.md names a base file
that must change.

What the base already has is configured, not rebuilt: access groups and the
segregation-of-duties rule, the credit check with notice and freeze days, sales extras
(check-ins, commissions, targets), giro, down payments, the sales and purchasing chains,
stock opname and transfers, minimum stock, fixed assets, the Coretax XML and legacy CSV,
bank statements and reconciliation, periods, the report catalogue, printing, master imports.

| # | Sub-project | What it adds | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Bootstrap (done 2026-10) | The base copied in, renamed Central, the client layer wired, CI and the session hook on Central's database names, this roadmap | `php artisan test` green on the copied base; the workspace shows "Central" |
| 1 | Branches and the order flow (done 2026-10) | Branches as cabang (code, coordinates), the branch in document numbers with a counter per branch; a team per customer (sales and marketing seats, Customer Teams); sales-order approval by the customer's marketing seat or an administrator; a reservations ledger (append-only, signed) written at approval under row locks, consumed by the delivery, released by an edit that reopens the approval, never expiring; the split of an order across warehouses from Order Approvals (home warehouse first, the branch's others, then the fullest, all pieces approved or none, `split_parent_id`) | An order with scattered goods approves as several pieces in one transaction, each reserved and numbered in its branch (`tests/Feature/Client`) |
| 2 | Price list (done 2026-10) | Price-list versions and items; imports with staged rows, the supplier-workbook parser (columns by position, categories from title rows, blockers vs notes), the five diff buckets, the safety brake with a typed acknowledgement, the full-replacement checkbox, export as `.xlsx` in the import's columns; one pricing seam (`Prices`) bound to `CentralPrices` (customer rules → tier rules → tier blanket discount → the version's list price → the base's price), its reason and version stamped on order lines at approval, an unpriced line stopping the approval | Export → edit HARGA → re-import publishes a new version; the brake stops a 25 % change until acknowledged; an order approved under one version keeps its prices after the next |
| 3 | Teams, claims, debt (done 2026-10) | The rights of the six roles as one matrix; two-key claims verified into base documents in the verifier's name: a settlement claim (pelunasan piutang → sales receipt by Finance), an expense claim (→ cash payment by Finance, on the expense account it picks), a return claim (→ sales return by Inventory; stock moves only then), the filer never verifying, the Owner included; the aging notice by email to the customer and the team at the notice days (120), once per invoice, the freeze (strictly after 150 from the issue date) staying the base's credit check; the collection desk (contacts, promises kept by later receipts, the worklist of invoices to chase); one active warehouse account per warehouse and the warehouse's fulfilment queue with Deliver | A claim filed by one person can only be verified by another; a customer with an invoice past 150 days cannot order until it settles |
| 4 | Buyer portal (done 2026-10) | A second panel `/portal` on a `customer` guard (`customer_users`, invited from Buyer Accounts, the invitation a signed set-password link), orders written in a seeded Portal system user's name with the buyer's login on them; the cart in the item's units priced by Central's rules with no price stored, the estimate with availability from the home warehouse and the free credit; checkout to an order awaiting approval (the freeze refuses, the amount limit warns and marketing decides), reorder from the home or any order; the catalogue with the buyer's prices and an availability badge; orders and invoices scoped to the buyer; the order, surat jalan and invoice as PDF from signed links; the credit strip and the aging banner above every page; the buyer's language on their profile | A buyer reorders last week's order in two clicks, sees their credit and their overdue invoices, and never sees another customer's document or the staff panel |
| 5 | Public site (done 2026-10) | Bilingual pages under Indonesian slugs (home, about, partners, roadmap, contact with every branch, two Indonesian-only legal pages, sign-in chooser), `/` the home instead of a redirect, the `bahasa` cookie set by a plain link, a second, stricter content policy (no inline script or style, nothing from another origin, no Livewire), robots and sitemap as routes; the company's copy as `id` / `en` pairs in the client config guarded by a test, overridden by the Owner on the Website screen (audited) and the home's promo carousel and photo gallery from Website Images (public disk, dates, order); no nearest-branch finder: a buyer's branch is the admin's choice, and the portal's availability badge now counts stock in every warehouse | Every page renders in both languages with no price anywhere; the legal pages stay Indonesian; a phone the Owner types on Website is on the contact page at once |
| 6 | Operations and deployment (done 2026-10) | `central:backup`: pg_dump and the kept files encrypted with libsodium's secretstream, written to the `s3` disk (any S3-compatible bucket), read back and decrypted before the run counts as verified, 14 daily and 12 monthly copies kept and never the newest; `central:restore --into` the drill, `--force` the live database; `central:health` (the database, Redis, the queue, failed jobs, the scheduler's heartbeat, the backup age, the disk; exit 0/1/2) and its hourly sweep mailing every active administrator plus an extra address once per critical incident, re-armed on recovery; `central:integrity` (journal balance and trial balance, item_costs against the movements, paid_amount against the allocations, reservations) nightly and mailed while a drift stands, nothing repaired; Launch Readiness: eleven automatic checks and six attested items, audited, `central:launch-check` exit 0 when nothing is open; the Operations screen; the deploy kit (`deploy/`: provision, deploy, Caddy, supervisor, logrotate) and the `production` branch workflow that runs the suite and then the script over SSH; DEPLOY.md rewritten as the runbook | A practice restore of last night's backup into a scratch database succeeds (the suite does it on every run); the launch check lists nothing open |
| 7 | Blue team (done 2026-10) | The defence of the one VPS: `deploy/harden.sh` (keys-only SSH with root locked out, `unattended-upgrades`, auditd on the files that decide access, CrowdSec with its firewall bouncer and a scenario fed by Central's own security log, Docker bound to loopback, the monitor site in Caddy); the monitoring stack under `deploy/monitoring/` (Grafana, Loki, Prometheus, Alloy and three exporters on the host network and loopback only, fourteen provisioned alert rules mailed to the owner, a root cron measuring unexpected listening ports and the certificate, a nightly log archive to the bucket with a write-only key); every sign-in attempt on both guards in the Activity Log and in a JSON security log; `central:security-watch` every ten minutes (passwords guessed per address and per account, an administrator from a new address, rights or memberships moved, Segregation of Duties off, a price list over the brake, a posted receipt changed, a night-time deletion), one report per six hours per subject; health and security alerts through one dispatcher, mail now and WhatsApp behind an interface once a provider is chosen; the Security card on Operations; BLUE-TEAM.md as the runbook | `harden.sh` and `install.sh` applied on the server; a failed panel login shows in Grafana and, past the threshold, in the administrators' mail; `ssh root@host` refused |

Not planned: a payment gateway, bank integrations, marketplace links, an app store,
financing programs, AI analysis, a mobile app, multi-currency, product reviews, a promo
or voucher engine, public price display.
